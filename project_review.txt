Overall

This is unusually disciplined embedded work. Single-writer data ownership is real and documented; the seqlock in rotor_control_runtime_get_snapshot (rotor_control_runtime.c:1001) is correctly written including the odd-sequence edge case; BASEPRI tiering is coherent; every controller validates its config and re-validates it on each step; wire payloads are length-checked before decode and range-checked twice (command handler and controller). The _Static_assert chain at main.c:1858-1869 that nests the current/velocity limits per layer is a genuinely good idea. I did not find a stub, a swallowed error, or a silent no-op in the product path.

The findings below are mostly about the next milestone, not the flashed one.

2. Electrical phase is zero-order-held at 1 kHz, with no lead compensation (high, for the 240 RPM envelope)        
motor_alignment_electrical_phase_q32 is computed from the raw angle alone (rotor_control_runtime.c:636-639), and   aligned_torque_controller.c:300-304 builds t 90° with no velocity term. At 50 electricalcycles/rev, the electrical angle advances 18° per sample at 1 rev/s but 72° per sample at 4 rev/s.                 
At the qualified 1 rev/s point that's a cos(9°) ≈ 1% torque loss — invisible. At the new 4 rev/s ceiling the vectorsweeps 72° across each hold interval: mean m injected d-axis current, and 1 kHz torqueripple. You'll likely read it as "the velocity loop won't track above ~2 rev/s" rather than as a phase problem.    
The DECISIONS entry anticipates this ("up to the point where the 1 kHz measured-phase refresh becomes the engineerilimitation"), so this is more where to look  T/2 lead to the phase handed tophase_current_reference_from_polar is cheap and buys most of it back; extrapolating phase in the 20 kHz backend is real fix.

5. The cascade has no rate headroom (medium, tuning)                                                               
POSITION_MAXIMUM_ACCELERATION_Q16_16 and VELOCITY_MAXIMUM_ACCELERATION_Q16_16 are both 4 << 16 (main.c:1826, :1833)The position controller emits profile.velociosition_controller.c:310-312), and thevelocity controller then slew-limits its own reference toward that at 4 rev/s² (velocity_controller.c:308-330).    
At max commanded acceleration the inner slew budget is fully consumed by the profile's own ramp, so the correction term gets applied with a first-order lag inson loop fights its own inner rate limiterexactly when following error is largest.
